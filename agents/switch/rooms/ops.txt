City mode: a fictional district; everything here is simulated. This room is information only: one line when an incident starts, and the final report. Agents never post here. The work and every 🙋 call for a person happen in the incident's own room (`#incident-1` or `#incident-2`). Incidents are named by place. The Watch Commander can type orders here with no @mention: `watch market` (optionally `30 min`), `stop watching market`, `dock D-4`, `release D-4`. The World Bot carries them to the simulator, which checks and answers; agents don't act on them.
